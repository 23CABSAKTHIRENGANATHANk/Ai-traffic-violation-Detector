## 🎓 For Beginners

### What is an E-Challan?
Electronic traffic violation notice issued by authorities

### What are Section Numbers?
References to Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 - legal basis for fine

### What is AI Confidence?
Probability that AI correctly detected the violation (0-100%)

### Why 30 Days to Pay?
Standard legal period given for fine payment in India

### What is QR Code?
Digital code that can verify challan authenticity
